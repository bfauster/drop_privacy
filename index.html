Privacy Policy

DROP! · Last updated: October 22, 2026

Privacy first. DROP! is designed to work offline. According to its current configuration, game progress and preferences are stored on your device, not sent to the developer's servers.

2. Information stored on your device

To make the game work, DROP! can save information locally on your device, such as:

  • Career progress, league results and game scores.
  • Highscores, achievements and statistics.
  • Character customization and game settings.
  • Daily challenge progress and locally saved rewards.

These records are used to maintain game progress and remember your preferences. They are stored locally in the app's storage area and are not intentionally transmitted to vster Studio.

3. Collection, sharing and third-party services

In its current version, DROP! does not require an account and does not include developer-operated cloud synchronization, advertising, analytics, online leaderboards or third-party tracking SDKs. We do not intentionally collect or share your gameplay information with third parties.

Your device operating system, app store and optional device backup services operate independently under their own privacy policies. Device backups, where enabled, may include app data depending on your device settings and platform configuration.

4. Storage, security and deletion

Locally saved information generally remains on your device until it is removed through the game's available reset options, the operating system's app-data controls or app uninstallation. The exact behavior can vary if device backup or restore is enabled.

DROP! relies on the security and access controls provided by the device operating system for its local app data. Because this game data is not transferred to our servers, we do not maintain a separate online copy to retrieve or delete.

5. Your choices and privacy rights

You can stop using DROP! at any time. You can also remove locally stored app data through your device settings, subject to any backups maintained by your device provider. If you have a privacy question, or wish to exercise rights that apply under data protection law, contact us using the details above. We will respond to requests relating to data under our control.

6. Children

DROP! does not intentionally request names, contact details or other personal information from players and does not offer in-game communication or account registration in its current version.

7. Changes to this policy

If DROP! introduces online features, advertising, analytics or other data processing in the future, this policy will be updated to describe those changes. The latest version will be available on this page.

8. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or DROP!, please contact vster.studio@gmx.net.
